Our Story

Haridwar Puja was established to preserve and promote authentic Vedic rituals and traditions. For generations, our family has been dedicated to performing sacred pujas and ceremonies on the holy banks of the Ganges River in Haridwar.

We believe in the power of faith, devotion, and tradition. Our experienced pandits bring centuries of knowledge and expertise to every ritual they perform. We are committed to maintaining the authenticity and sanctity of Vedic practices while making them accessible to devotees worldwide.

Today, through our online platform, we are able to serve devotees across the globe who seek authentic spiritual experiences and connection with their roots.
