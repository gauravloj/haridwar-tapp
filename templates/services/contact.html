Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 7 days in advance. However, we can accommodate urgent bookings with additional service charges.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets through our secure Razorpay payment gateway.

Can I reschedule my booking?

Yes, you can reschedule your booking with at least 7 days notice. Contact our team for rescheduling details.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Yes, cancellations made 15 days before the ritual date are eligible for a full refund. Contact support for details.
